## Customer-Facing Transparency & Data Safety

### What customers see (transparent):
- Item-by-item quote breakdown (quantity × unit price = line total)
- Bulk discount tier and amount saved
- Subtotal → discount → final total price
- Estimated delivery timeline
- Reasons for unfulfilled items (out of stock, not in catalog, deadline missed)
- Professional, customer-friendly `quote_explanation`

### What is NEVER exposed to customers:
- Supplier cost ratio (70% of catalog price) — internal margin
- Exact cash balance or reserve threshold — internal financials
- Raw system errors or stack traces — internal PII/debug info
- Supplier lead-time formulas — internal logistics
- Database schema or table names — internal infrastructure
